Governments have confirmed that unidentified aerial phenomena are real, unexplained, and a national security concern. This is official. It has been stated in published reports, in congressional testimony, and in the establishment of dedicated investigation offices with statutory authority.

No government has officially confirmed that any UAP is extraterrestrial in origin. The distinction between those two statements matters enormously. "We can't identify it" is Tier 1. "It's alien" is Tier 3.

## What Has Actually Been Officially Confirmed

The U.S. government has released multiple official reports on UAP. The Director of National Intelligence has published findings acknowledging unexplained cases. The Department of Defense has established and funded dedicated UAP investigation infrastructure. Members of Congress have passed legislation requiring systematic reporting on UAP military encounters.

A former senior intelligence officer testified under oath to Congress that the U.S. government possesses non-human intelligence material and retrieved craft. That testimony was given under penalty of perjury and carries legal weight. The underlying claims have not been independently confirmed or officially corroborated.

## The Tier System Applied

**Tier 1 — Officially confirmed:**
- UAP exist as a documented category of unexplained objects
- The U.S. military has tracked them on multiple independent sensor systems simultaneously
- Official reports have been published acknowledging unexplained cases that resist conventional explanation
- Congressional hearings have been held and legislation has passed requiring ongoing reporting
- A dedicated government office (AARO) has been established with statutory authority
- Declassified infrared footage has been authenticated by the Department of Defense

**Tier 2 — Claimed under oath, not independently confirmed:**
- David Grusch testified under oath to the existence of a classified program to recover and reverse-engineer non-human craft
- Additional witnesses have described compartmentalized programs with access controls above standard classification levels
- These claims carry legal weight because of the oath context. They do not, by themselves, constitute official confirmation.

**Tier 3 — Not admitted, not confirmed:**
- Official government statement that any specific UAP is extraterrestrial in origin
- Publicly available physical evidence of non-human technology
- Confirmed communication with non-human intelligence

## The Careful Language of Officials

The language used in official UAP contexts is deliberate and worth analyzing. Officials describe "objects of unknown origin" rather than "alien craft." Reports note that unexplained cases cannot be attributed to U.S. programs or known foreign adversaries — rather than stating non-human origin.

When congressional questioners press for specifics, they are sometimes directed to classified settings. When officials speak publicly about what they believe, they are careful to distinguish personal assessment from official position.

That careful language is itself data. It suggests officials are either genuinely uncertain, legally constrained in what they can confirm publicly, or both.

## The Grusch Testimony: A Specific Assessment

David Grusch is a former senior intelligence official who served on the UAP task force. He testified under oath to Congress that the United States government has operated a multi-decade program to recover and reverse-engineer non-human technology, and that this program has been illegally withheld from congressional oversight.

The specificity and the oath context give this testimony weight that anonymous leaks do not have. Grusch has stated he has provided classified evidence to the relevant Inspector General.

The investigation is ongoing. His claims remain unconfirmed by independent corroboration. They are Tier 2 — credible, specific, legally sworn, and unverified.
