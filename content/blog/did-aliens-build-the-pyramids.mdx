No. Humans built the pyramids. We know this because we found the workers' villages, the tools, the administrative records, the quarry sites, the construction ramps, and the graffiti left by the named work gangs who did the work.

The ancient astronaut theory requires ignoring that evidence. A Tier 1 archaeological record does not support a Tier 3 interpretation.

## What We Actually Found

The workers who built the Great Pyramid at Giza were not slaves. Excavations of a workers' settlement at the site revealed organized villages with medical facilities, bread and beer production at industrial scale, and evidence of workers who received treatment for injuries — including healed fractures that indicate a valued labor force with access to care.

The Merer Papyrus — the oldest papyrus ever discovered — is a logbook written by an overseer of one of the work crews. It describes transporting limestone blocks from a quarry to Giza. It is a record of logistics. It is not alien.

## The Tier System Applied

**Tier 1 — Documented fact:** Worker settlements have been excavated and dated to the construction period. Administrative records exist in papyrus form. Copper tools consistent with the construction have been found at quarry sites. Experimental archaeology has demonstrated that large stone blocks can be moved by organized human labor using wooden sledges, water lubrication, and ramp systems — these methods are documented and have been replicated.

**Tier 2 — Strongly suggested, unproven:** The precise construction sequence for the upper courses — how the capstone was placed, the exact ramp geometry — is still debated among engineers and archaeologists. The astronomical precision of the site alignment is clearly intentional, but the exact method of achieving it is not yet definitively established.

**Tier 3 — Speculative:** Alien or advanced non-human assistance. Lost civilization technology. Any Technospermia-adjacent connection through pyramid construction. These are not supported by the archaeological record.

## Why the Theory Persists

The ancient astronaut theory persists because the pyramids are genuinely astonishing. The precision, the scale, the logistics — it is tempting to reach for an explanation that matches the apparent enormity of the achievement.

The problem is that the documentation of how it was done is better evidence than the architectural analysis alone. The "impossible for humans" framing is based on underestimating ancient organizational capacity and engineering ingenuity, not on missing evidence.

When you actually read the Merer Papyrus, you find a middle manager tracking his crew's work schedule and material deliveries. The pyramids were a management challenge, not an alien construction project.
