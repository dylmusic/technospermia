Some death questions are settled. Some are genuinely open in the academic literature. Some are speculative in the technical sense — seriously entertained by researchers but not yet evidenced. And some questions that seem profound are actually about definitions rather than facts.

Knowing which category a question falls into doesn't make death less mysterious. But it prevents us from feeling falsely certain when we aren't, and from feeling falsely ignorant when we aren't that either.

## What Science Has Established

**The physiological process of dying** is well-understood and not in dispute. This includes the sequence of events from cessation of circulation through the end of organized brain activity.

**NDEs as reported experiences** are established — they are real, consistent, measurable, and associated with lasting change. This is not a contested scientific claim.

**The phenomenological overlap** between NDEs and high-dose psychedelic experiences is documented across independent research programs.

**Terminal lucidity** is a documented medical phenomenon, reported across more than a century of independent accounts and acknowledged in peer-reviewed palliative care literature.

**Ancient and cross-cultural psychedelic use** has been confirmed by chemical residue analysis.

None of this establishes survival of consciousness. All of it creates the context in which that question deserves serious rather than dismissive attention.

## What Remains Genuinely Open

**Whether NDEs reflect consciousness independent of brain activity** — veridical-perception cases are the crux; the evidence is suggestive and contested.

**The mechanism of terminal lucidity** — no current explanation accounts cleanly for all documented cases.

**What consciousness is** — the hard problem is unsolved, which means the death question it depends on is downstream-unsolved too.

**Whether any aspect of consciousness persists after death** — survival research is ongoing; no established conclusion in either direction.

## What Remains Speculative

**Survival of consciousness** — seriously researched, not established.

**Personal identity continuing after death** — a narrower and more specific claim than bare survival; even more difficult to confirm.

**Specific post-death states** — heaven, reincarnation, enlightenment, merger with a cosmic consciousness — these specific claims are further from current evidence than the bare survival question.

**The Technospermia theory's specific claims** about consciousness being fundamental and seeded — coherent, internally consistent, Tier 3.
