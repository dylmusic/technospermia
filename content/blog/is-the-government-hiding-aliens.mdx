Governments confirmed they tracked, investigated, and withheld information about unexplained aerial phenomena while simultaneously issuing public statements dismissing the subject. That documented gap between private investigation and public denial is Tier 1 fact.

Whether what they were tracking includes extraterrestrial craft is a completely different question. One that has not been officially answered.

"They were concealing something" is confirmed. "They were concealing aliens" is an inference the evidence doesn't yet support.

## The Documented Pattern of Concealment

Declassified documents confirm that military and intelligence agencies ran formal programs to investigate unexplained aerial incidents while simultaneously characterizing public interest in the subject as unscientific or confused.

This gap — serious private investigation alongside public dismissal — is the part that is confirmed. What the investigation found, and what remains undisclosed, is where the genuine uncertainty begins.

## The Tier System Applied

**Tier 1 — Documented fact:** Programs to investigate UAP existed and operated while public statements dismissed the subject. Declassified documents confirm these programs. Official reports now acknowledge a residual of unexplained cases. Congressional hearings have been held with government witnesses. A statutory oversight office now exists with mandatory reporting requirements.

**Tier 2 — Alleged under oath, not independently confirmed:** Former officials have testified to the existence of recovery and reverse-engineering programs operating outside normal oversight channels. These allegations are specific, made under penalty of perjury, and have not been independently corroborated by released physical evidence.

**Tier 3 — Speculative:** That the undisclosed material includes extraterrestrial craft or biological remains. That the concealment is specifically protecting knowledge of non-human intelligence. These are coherent inferences from the documented pattern. They are not conclusions the evidence supports yet.

## Why Secrecy Has Multiple Explanations

Unexplained objects operating near sensitive military installations are a security concern regardless of their origin. Protecting sensor capabilities, preserving adversary uncertainty about what the military can track, avoiding public disruption — all are documented institutional motivations for non-disclosure that require no extraterrestrial explanation.

"They hid it because it's alien" is one reading. "They hid it the way they hide most classified programs" is another. The first is not ruled out. The second requires no extraordinary assumptions.

Both explanations can be true simultaneously for different parts of the concealment. The alien explanation is not required to explain the secrecy. The secrecy is not proof of the alien explanation.
