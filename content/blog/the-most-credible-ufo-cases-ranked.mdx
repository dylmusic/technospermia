The UFO case library runs to thousands of incidents. Most deserve little weight — single witnesses, no corroborating data, no official investigation, no physical evidence. A much smaller set deserves serious attention because the evidence for them is genuinely strong.

This guide evaluates the most important cases by what they actually establish, ranked not by how dramatic the claimed encounter is, but by how well the evidence holds up to scrutiny.

## The Tier 1 Cases — Best Evidence

### The Nimitz Encounter

The gold standard of the modern UAP evidence record. Multi-day radar tracking, visual confirmation by trained combat pilots, and officially released infrared footage combine to produce the most rigorously documented case in the contemporary period. The lack of any publicly offered conventional explanation that accounts for all elements of the case is significant.

Full account: [The Nimitz Tic-Tac Encounter](/blog/the-nimitz-tic-tac-encounter)

### Rendlesham Forest

The UK's most documented case. The deputy base commander's official memo and his contemporaneous audio recording made during the event are primary source documents of unusual quality. Whatever Halt and his team experienced, they documented it as they experienced it.

Full account: [The Rendlesham Forest Incident](/blog/the-rendlesham-forest-incident)

### The Belgian UFO Wave

The only case in the modern record where a national military investigated a sustained multi-witness UAP phenomenon, scrambled fighters, gathered radar and pilot data, and then published a report acknowledging it could not explain the phenomenon. Institutional candor of this kind is historically rare.

Full account: [The Belgian UFO Wave](/blog/the-belgian-ufo-wave)

## The Tier 2 Cases — Strong Witness, Limited Corroboration

### The Phoenix Lights (V-Formation)

Thousands of witnesses across multiple cities describe a consistent, large, silent, structured formation. The geographic distribution of independent witnesses along a flight corridor makes simple mass misidentification difficult to sustain. The second event (stationary orbs) has a credible explanation; the V-formation does not.

Full account: [The Phoenix Lights Explained](/blog/the-phoenix-lights-explained)

### The Westall Sighting

Hundreds of simultaneous daylight witnesses from two schools, observed the same object under conditions that minimize the factors enabling simple dismissal. No official explanation has ever been offered — not a wrong explanation, no explanation at all.

Full account: [The Westall UFO Sighting](/blog/the-westall-ufo-sighting)

### The Aguadilla Video

Infrared footage analyzed by independent technical researchers who concluded the object's behavior — including apparent trans-medium capability — cannot be accounted for by known aircraft types. Has not been officially addressed.

Full account: [The Aguadilla UFO Video](/blog/the-aguadilla-ufo-video)

## The Contested Cases — Significant Witness, Methodological Complications

### The Travis Walton Abduction

Six independent witnesses to the event that preceded Walton's disappearance, a confirmed five-day missing person period, and multiple polygraph administrations. The strongest multi-witness abduction account in the record, though abduction cases carry inherent verification challenges.

Full account: [The Travis Walton Abduction](/blog/the-travis-walton-abduction)

### The Betty and Barney Hill Abduction

The origin point of the modern abduction narrative. Established by a credible psychiatrist using standard methodology. The cultural influence of this case means everything after it occurs in its shadow — which complicates evaluation of subsequent accounts.

Full account: [The Betty and Barney Hill Abduction](/blog/the-betty-and-barney-hill-abduction)

### The Pascagoula Abduction

Unusual for the hidden recording evidence — two men continued describing their experience when they believed they were alone and unobserved. The sheriff's assessment was that they appeared to genuinely believe what they were reporting.

Full account: [The Pascagoula Abduction](/blog/the-pascagoula-abduction)

### The Zimbabwe Ariel School Encounter

Approximately 62 children describing the same beings and the same apparent communication without a prior cultural template for the specific account. John Mack's investigation found the testimony credible. Child witness cases carry reliability complications alongside the unusual characteristics of the testimony.

Full account: [The Zimbabwe Ariel School Encounter](/blog/the-zimbabwe-ariel-school-encounter)

## The Recovery Cases — Physical Objects, Official Complications

### The Roswell Incident

The most famous case in the record. The documented core — contradictory official press statements, debris recovery, a classified balloon program — is real and interesting. The alien bodies and craft accounts emerged decades later and cannot be confirmed. The case is better and worse than its reputation simultaneously.

Full account: [The Roswell Incident Explained](/blog/the-roswell-incident-explained)

### The Kecksburg Incident

Multiple local witnesses describing a physical object and a military recovery, with official records stating nothing was found, and a NASA FOIA investigation that produced missing files. The witness-to-official-record gap is documented and persistent.

Full account: [The Kecksburg Incident](/blog/the-kecksburg-incident)

## The Institutional Cases — Official Programs and Their Records

### Bob Lazar and Area 51

Small verifiable details that survived scrutiny surrounding core claims that cannot be independently confirmed. The credential questions are a genuine problem; the verified details are genuinely interesting. Neither resolves the other.

Full account: [Bob Lazar and Area 51](/blog/bob-lazar-and-area-51)

### What Was the Condon Report?

The scientific study commissioned to close Project Blue Book — which contained significant unexplained cases within its own findings while reaching a headline conclusion that further study was unwarranted. The internal inconsistency between the findings and the conclusion is documented.

Full account: [What Was the Condon Report?](/blog/what-was-the-condon-report)

## The Standard This Library Uses

Every case in this guide is assessed against the same framework: what is documented fact, what is strongly suggested but unproven, and what is speculative. The ranking above reflects evidence quality — not dramatic impact or cultural prominence.

Cases that are famous are not automatically credible. Cases that are less famous are not automatically weak. The evidence is what matters, and the strongest cases are the ones where multiple independent evidence types converge on an unexplained observation.
