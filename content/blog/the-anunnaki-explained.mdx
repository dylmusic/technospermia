The Anunnaki existed — as the major divine beings of ancient Mesopotamian religion, appearing in Sumerian, Akkadian, Babylonian, and Assyrian texts across millennia. They were worshipped, petitioned, feared, and celebrated in some of the earliest written literature in human history.

The version of the Anunnaki widely circulated today — alien astronauts from a planet called Nibiru who genetically engineered humanity — comes almost entirely from one man's books. Professional scholars of ancient languages have uniformly rejected his translations.

## Who the Anunnaki Actually Were

In Sumerian and later Mesopotamian religion, the Anunnaki were a group of major deities — divine beings associated with the sky, earth, and underworld. They appear in texts as powerful, sometimes capricious beings who interacted with humans through temples and rituals. They were not a monolithic group with a single clear identity; their characterization varied across regions and periods.

They feature prominently in the Epic of Gilgamesh, in the Descent of Inanna, and in numerous creation and flood narratives. They were worshipped in temple complexes and their favor was sought through sacrifice and ritual.

None of this is disputed. It is documented across thousands of cuneiform tablets from sites across the ancient Near East.

## The Tier System Applied

**Tier 1 — Documented fact:** The Anunnaki appear as major deities in Sumerian, Akkadian, Babylonian, and Assyrian religious texts, documented across thousands of cuneiform tablets spanning thousands of years. The ancient Mesopotamian religion in which they figure is extensively documented and actively studied by professional Assyriologists. Zecharia Sitchin's interpretations of these texts — claiming the Anunnaki were alien colonizers from a planet called Nibiru — have been uniformly rejected by professional scholars who specialize in ancient Near Eastern languages.

**Tier 2 — Genuine open questions:** Some specific aspects of Mesopotamian cosmology and the earlier evolution of the Anunnaki concept across different Mesopotamian cultures remain areas of active scholarship. These are detailed questions about the development of religious concepts, not mysteries about alien origin.

**Tier 3 — Speculative:** That the Anunnaki were literal extraterrestrial beings. That Sitchin's translations are correct despite expert rejection. That the Mesopotamian creation narratives describe genetic engineering of humans by alien colonizers. These are Sitchin's claims; they are not supported by the texts as read by people trained to read them.

## What Makes the Actual Mesopotamian Texts Worth Reading

The Sumerian and Akkadian texts are extraordinary on their own terms. The Epic of Gilgamesh contains a flood narrative potentially older than the Biblical version, philosophical reflections on mortality and friendship, and an early example of the hero's journey structure. The Descent of Inanna is a profound piece of ancient religious literature.

The Anunnaki within these texts are complex divine beings — not always sympathetic, sometimes in conflict with each other, sometimes protective and sometimes punishing toward humans. They reflect a sophisticated theological imagination.

That imagination deserves to be read as what it was — a genuine ancient human attempt to understand the cosmos, divine power, and the human place in creation — rather than as a distorted record of an alien colonization event.
