There is no credible evidence that reptilian beings control human civilization. The modern conspiracy theory — popularized by David Icke — that shape-shifting reptilians occupy positions of global power has no factual basis and has been extensively documented to recycle centuries-old antisemitic tropes about hidden elites. This article rejects that framing explicitly and entirely.

What is genuinely worth examining — as a question about human neuroscience and consciousness — is why reptilian imagery recurs across unrelated folklore traditions and in psychedelic entity encounters. That is a different question. And it has a more interesting answer.

## The Documented Phenomenon: A Recurring Motif

Serpent and reptilian figures appear in the mythologies of cultures that had no documented contact with each other — Mesopotamian, Mesoamerican, South Asian, Sub-Saharan African, Australian Aboriginal. The motif is not evidence of literal beings. It is evidence of something consistent about human symbol-making and perception.

The same motif appears in psychedelic entity reports. People in high-dose DMT and ayahuasca experiences sometimes describe entities as reptilian, insectoid, or serpentine. This is documented in formal research contexts.

These are real data points about human consciousness. They are not evidence of a hidden ruling class.

## The Tier System Applied

**Tier 1 — Documented fact:** The reptilian and serpent motif recurs cross-culturally in mythology and art, across populations with no historical contact. Psychedelic entity encounter reports include reptilian beings at a documented rate. Primate neuroscience has identified dedicated threat-detection circuitry for serpents — the snake-detection hypothesis, developed by primatologist Lynne Isbell, proposes that primate visual cortex complexity evolved partly in response to serpent predation pressure. The modern conspiracy theory exists and has been documented to carry antisemitic structural features.

**Tier 2 — Nothing credible.** This tier is empty for reptilians. There is no strongly-suggested-but-unproven evidence for literal reptilian beings. The mid-tier requires some evidential foundation. There is none here.

**Tier 3 — Speculative and not internally coherent:** Literal shape-shifting beings occupying positions of power. The theory has not demonstrated internal coherence when subjected to basic evidentiary standards. It belongs in Tier 3 with the caveat that even that tier requires the hypothesis to be coherent — and this one has serious problems.

The snake-detection hypothesis proposes that ancestral primates faced significant serpent predation pressure, and that visual cortex complexity — and specifically pattern-recognition circuits tuned for serpentine forms — evolved partly as a response. If accurate, this means the human brain carries ancient hardware that prioritizes detecting reptilian shapes. That hardware may generate reptilian imagery in dreams, altered states, and hypnagogic experiences without any external beings present.

## The Antisemitism Problem — Addressed Directly

The modern reptilian conspiracy theory requires explicit engagement on this point. Scholars studying conspiracy theory structure — including analysts at universities and institutions specializing in extremism research — have documented that the framework of a shape-shifting, secret, elite group controlling governments and financial systems mirrors and recycles medieval and early modern antisemitic conspiracy structures.

The theory does not avoid this problem by substituting "reptilians" for any ethnic or religious group. The structural logic — the hidden bloodline, the infiltration of institutions, the control of money — is the same. Promoting this framework in any form that maps onto ethnic or religious identity is not edgy speculation. It is the recycling of documented hate tropes with a science-fiction veneer.

This site rejects that framing. Understanding why reptilian imagery recurs in human consciousness requires separating the neuroscience question entirely from that lineage.
