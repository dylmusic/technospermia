The Aguadilla video shows an unidentified object tracked in infrared over northwestern Puerto Rico near Rafael Hernández Airport. What distinguishes it from many UAP videos is a specific sequence: the object appears to enter the ocean and then reemerge on the other side of the water — traveling through both air and water at significant speed without visible change in flight characteristics.

This sequence, if accurately captured, is not consistent with any publicly known aircraft type.

## What the Footage Shows

The video was recorded by the infrared camera system aboard a U.S. Customs and Border Protection aircraft. The camera tracked an unlit object traveling at speed over the airport, along the coast, and then apparently into the ocean. The object appeared to reemerge from the water and continue on its course.

The object in the infrared footage has no visible wings, no visible exhaust signature, and produces no visible wake effect when apparently transiting through water. It passed near an active runway approach path during its initial flight track.

The footage was not released officially. It was obtained and analyzed by researchers at the Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies, a group of academics and analysts who applied technical examination to the video using standard methodologies from aviation and physics.

## Why the Absence of Official Response Matters

The Aguadilla footage documents an event that occurred near an active airport approach path. If the object was an unauthorized drone or aircraft, it represented a serious airspace safety violation in a sensitive area. The absence of any official response — no investigation announced, no explanation offered, no law enforcement action described — is itself unusual for an object that clearly operated in controlled airspace.

This absence of official engagement is not evidence of a cover-up. It may reflect that the footage was obtained informally and did not enter official channels in a way that triggered response. But for a near-airport airspace incursion visible on multiple platforms, the lack of any public official record is notable.
