Calvin Parker and Charles Hickson were fishing on the Pascagoula River in Mississippi when they reported an encounter that would become one of the most investigated abduction cases in the United States. What gives this case an unusual evidentiary characteristic is not what they reported — it is what they did immediately afterward and what happened when they believed they were alone.

They went to the sheriff's office. And then the sheriff tried to catch them lying.

## The Reported Encounter

The two men were fishing at a dock on the Pascagoula River when they reported hearing a whirring or zipping noise and seeing a bluish, oval-shaped light descend near them. They described a craft with an opening that emitted a bright light.

They then described being floated or levitated toward the craft by three beings — robotic in appearance, with claw-like hands, no eyes or nose in the conventional sense, and a slit-like mouth. Inside, Hickson described being examined by a device that passed over his body. Parker reported blacking out. They found themselves back on the riverbank.

## The Tier System Applied

**Tier 1 — Documented fact:** Two men went to the Jackson County Sheriff's office and reported an unusual experience on the river. The sheriff conducted an interview and placed a hidden recording device in the room. The two men were recorded continuing to describe the experience when they believed they were alone. Both men's accounts were consistent when interviewed separately. Hickson agreed to a polygraph examination. The case was investigated by researchers at the time and has been examined in subsequent investigations.

**Tier 2 — Suggested, not confirmed:** The consistency of the two men's accounts under separate questioning, the hidden recording showing they continued their account without knowing they were observed, and the sheriff's assessment that they appeared to genuinely believe what they were reporting all suggest the men were not straightforwardly fabricating a story for attention or gain.

**Tier 3 — Speculative:** That the beings and the craft they described were literally present. That what they experienced was a physical interaction with non-human entities. These claims describe what the men reported experiencing; they are not confirmed by independent evidence.

## What Happened to Calvin Parker

Calvin Parker remained largely out of public view for decades after the event. He later gave interviews and wrote about the experience. His subsequent descriptions have been consistent with the original account in their broad outlines, though like all memory, details across accounts over long periods carry the inherent issues of long-term recall.

He has described the experience as profoundly disruptive to his life and has expressed ongoing distress associated with it. His eventual willingness to speak publicly about it — after decades of silence — does not on its own confirm what happened. But it is part of the documented record.
